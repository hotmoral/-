# Timing Defense — Class Skill Plan Lv6–31

Common automatic unlock levels:
`Lv6 / Lv11 / Lv16 / Lv21 / Lv26 / Lv31`

## Knight — generalist / penetration / heavy finisher
- Lv6 **Enhanced Blade Wave**: all blade-wave damage +12%, projectile size +25%.
- Lv11 **Breakthrough Slash**: normal blade wave penetrates 1 additional enemy at 85% damage; heavy penetrates 2 additional enemies.
- Lv16 **Sharp Edge**: attacks containing PERFECT gain +18% damage and +20% knockback.
- Lv21 **Great Blade Wave**: heavy attack becomes a much larger crescent wave, +20% heavy damage, +55% visual width, up to 3 additional penetrations.
- Lv26 **Frontline Collapse**: penetration no longer decays in damage; each penetration increases knockback against the next enemy by +15%.
- Lv31 **Heaven-Splitting Slash**: P×P CRITICAL produces a huge double blade wave with broad penetration and major knockback.

## Rogue — multihit / crit / high-HP normal execution
- Lv6 **Double Dagger**: second dagger deals 55% of the primary hit.
- Lv11 **Weak-Point Detection**: +6 percentage points class crit chance.
- Lv16 **Assassin Rhythm**: PERFECT reduces the next attack cycle delay by 20%.
- Lv21 **Blade Barrage**: heavy becomes 5 daggers: center 100%, four follow-ups 45% each.
- Lv26 **Chain Throw**: PERFECT creates an additional tracking dagger against the nearest secondary enemy for 45% damage.
- Lv31 **Dagger Storm**: P×P CRITICAL launches 8 rapid tracking daggers over roughly 0.5 s.

Important rule: no same-target hit cap. Bosses instead take reduced dagger damage; initial scalar = 0.70.

## Tank — knockback / wall protection / crowd control
- Lv6 **Impact Wave+**: shockwave width +30%, knockback +30%.
- Lv11 **Iron Wall**: wall incoming damage -10%; does not stack across multiple tanks.
- Lv16 **Perfect Guard**: PERFECT grants an 80-damage wall shield for 3 seconds; refreshes duration/value, does not stack.
- Lv21 **Ground Impact**: heavy becomes a broad frontal shockwave with approximately ×2.0 knockback and multi-target coverage.
- Lv26 **Aftershock Zone**: heavy leaves a 1.5 s zone that slows enemy movement by 25%.
- Lv31 **Absolute Barrier**: P×P CRITICAL creates a battlefield-wide shockwave and grants a 250 wall shield for 6 seconds.

Boss knockback initial scalar = 0.40.

## Archer — straight-line penetration / rear-target deletion
- Lv6 **Piercing Arrow**: projectile can penetrate up to 2 additional enemies.
- Lv11 **Strong Bow**: projectile speed +25%, base damage +10%.
- Lv16 **Precision Penetration**: PERFECT arrows ignore armor and lose no damage through penetration.
- Lv21 **Triple Shot**: heavy becomes three tightly spaced arrows: 100% + 65% + 65%.
- Lv26 **Impaling Shot**: PERFECT penetration count +2 and preserves 100% rear-target damage.
- Lv31 **Sky-Piercing Arrow**: P×P CRITICAL launches a huge high-speed arrow that penetrates the battlefield without target-count limit and ignores armor.

## Mage — area damage / density scaling / chain explosion
- Lv6 **Arcane Burst**: impact causes 50% splash damage around the target.
- Lv11 **Expanded Magic Circle**: burst radius +35%, splash damage 50% → 60%.
- Lv16 **Resonance**: PERFECT creates a second 55% outer-ring explosion after ~0.18 s. The original center target is excluded from the second explosion so Mage stays a crowd specialist rather than becoming a boss DPS specialist.
- Lv21 **Greater Arcane Orb**: heavy becomes a slower, larger projectile with ×1.6 explosion radius and +20% damage.
- Lv26 **Chain Explosion**: when an explosion catches at least 2 enemies, secondary explosions propagate to up to 2 enemies for 35% damage.
- Lv31 **Arcane Collapse**: P×P CRITICAL creates a large magic circle, three-stage blast and chained secondary explosions.

## Role target at Lv31
- Knight: splits and destabilizes the frontline.
- Rogue: overwhelms durable normal targets with repeated dagger hits.
- Tank: prevents enemies from reaching the wall.
- Archer: erases lines of enemies with penetration.
- Mage: turns dense enemy groups into cascading explosions.
