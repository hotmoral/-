# 1. Approved EXP source semantics

Baseline EXP source:
- one valid completed **local timing turn**;
- no kill EXP;
- no raw-damage EXP;
- no passive/AI EXP;
- no flat Stage-clear EXP;
- no hidden class-specific catch-up EXP;
- empty/no-target turns cannot farm EXP;
- an already-valid started turn may resolve/reward under the existing target-loss rule.

This preserves timing quality as the progression source.

---

# 2. Approved turn-level award semantics

Normal timing turn:
- one resolved NORMAL / GOOD / PERFECT reward.

Heavy timing turn:
- X + Y resolve to one combined Heavy tier;
- exactly one EXP award is generated for the completed Heavy turn.

Heavy X and Y do not independently award EXP in the P4 baseline.

# 3. Approved calibration unit

P4 calibrates in dimensionless `XP_CREDIT`, not player-visible integer EXP.

Primary candidate relative quality vector:

```text
NORMAL    1.000
GOOD      1.500
PERFECT   2.250
HEAVY     1.250
HEAVY+    1.750
GREAT     2.250
SUPER     2.875
CRITICAL  3.500
```

This vector is the primary calibration candidate because it preserves the legacy relative quality shape while discarding obsolete absolute scale.

Gentler/stronger quality sensitivity lanes may be tested, but no vector is numerically frozen by this contract.

Player-visible EXP numbers are deferred until the reach structure is accepted.

# 8. Approved universal-curve policy

Use one universal run-level EXP curve across all five classes and current Tiers.

Baseline prohibits:
- class-specific EXP multipliers;
- Tier-specific hidden XP multipliers;
- automatic catch-up solely because a class kills faster.

Observed feedback:

```text
more player power
 -> faster kills
 -> fewer local timing turns
 -> fewer later XP opportunities
```

is treated initially as measurable self-damping, not an automatic defect.

If a class/Tier is structurally pushed outside intended windows:
1. inspect encounter composition / target availability;
2. inspect whether Stage identity can be adjusted naturally through monster count, durability, armor, cadence, formation or pressure composition without flattening Tier identity;
3. inspect milestone/skill-power interaction;
4. classify healthy self-damping vs structural progression failure;
5. only then consider a general/visible stabilizer through a new decision gate.

EXP must not force all Tiers into identical encounter flow.
Encounter identity remains primary; progression and encounter opportunity are calibrated to converge without homogenization.
